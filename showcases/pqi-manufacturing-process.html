About this showcase. Sakura Bloom is a synthetic product from the ISO/TC 215/SC 2/WG 1 working draft of ISO 26060 (WD stage, 2026-11-01). The draft is not an International Standard and will change. The per-tablet composition assumes the 52.000 kg of Sakuramil per batch makes 2,600,000 tablets of 20 mg with no overage. ISO 26060 classes and individuals use example.org placeholder URIs until ISO assigns official identifiers.
