Manufacturing, step by step

The manufacturing processes of Sakura Bloom 20 mg, played back from their FHIR PQI process descriptions. Move the slider to follow one batch: starting materials react to form the drug substance Sakuramil, the drug substance and excipients are blended, granulated, compressed and coated into tablets, and the tablets are inspected and packaged. The panel shows the structured data for each step: materials and charges, equipment, process parameters and controls.

    Illustrative: equipment and materials are drawn schematically, and particle counts do not represent quantities. Every step, material, charge, item of equipment, parameter and control comes from the PlanDefinition process descriptions of the synthetic Sakura Bloom dossier (CTD 3.2.S.2.2 and 3.2.P.3.3). The product, companies and data are fictitious.
