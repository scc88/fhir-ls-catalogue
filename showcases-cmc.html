Demonstrated in the showcase

01Navigation

Navigate the CTD as one connected document

Browse Module 2.3 and 3.2 as a single searchable tree. Every section shows the structured data behind its narrative, and any datum opens in place with its FHIR source.

“Show me the data behind 2.3.3.DP.C.”

02Conformance

Check every result against its limit automatically

All 1,443 results are evaluated against the acceptance range of the test they instantiate, with release and shelf-life contexts applied correctly. Results nearest a limit surface first.

“Is anything out of specification or trending towards a limit?”

Review specifications as data

04Stability

Trend stability data against the shelf-life limit

Plot any attribute by batch, pack and storage condition from the registration studies, with the limit drawn and a simple trend line to 36 months. No retyping of stability tables.

09Post-approval

Triage post-approval changes received by API

A sponsor posts a FHIR PQI transaction Bundle. The agency compares it with the approved dossier, proposes a reporting category under Health Canada’s Post-NOC Changes: Quality guidance (escalating when a condition is not met), and places it on a low-to-high risk gradient for routing.

“Is this a Supplement, an Annual Notification or a Record of Change — and how risky is it?”
